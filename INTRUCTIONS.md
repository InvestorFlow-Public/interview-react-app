# Interview Assigment

As the DevOps team responsible for deploying the application and infrastructure, your role is crucial. The repository InvestorFlow-Public/interview-react-app needs its application deployed, which will require your expertise in building pipelines and implementing new Infrastructure as Code (IaC).

## Please consider the following:

### Multi-environment infrastructure (dev, qa, stg, and prd).

- Azure-only infrastructure.
- DRY principles.
- Static code scanning.
- PR integrations.

We don't expect you to actually deploy resources at this stage. Instead, we'd like to see your outputs, example deployment steps, and overall approach to demonstrate your skills and best practices.

GitHub should be used for this demonstration, and Terraform is expected as the IaC tool.

## Interview Structure

- 10–15 mins – Demo and presentation of your DevOps implementation.
- 15 mins – Q&A from interviewers about the implementation.
- 15 mins – Q&A on DevOps topics.
- 15 mins – Your questions and closing.

Please send your completed work and any materials you'd like to share before the interview. You will receive a calendar invitation with the confirmed interview time.
